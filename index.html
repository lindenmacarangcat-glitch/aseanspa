About the Spa

At ASEAN Home Service Massage and Spa, we believe that true wellness begins with a moment of peace. Our sanctuary offers a harmonious blend of traditional Filipino hospitality and contemporary massage techniques, creating a space where stress melts away and balance is restored.

Founded with a passion for holistic healing, our journey started with a simple belief: that everyone deserves access to professional, high-quality massage therapy in an environment that feels like home. Today, we bring this vision to life through our spa, home, and hotel services across the Philippines.

What sets us apart is our unwavering commitment to personalized care. From the moment you book, to the final moments of your session, we tailor every experience to your unique needs — ensuring each visit leaves you feeling renewed, centered, and deeply cared for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept walk-ins?
Yes, we welcome walk-ins at our spa location. However, we recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time and therapist.
Do I need an appointment?
Appointments are highly recommended to ensure availability, especially for home and hotel services. We accept same-day bookings with at least 2 hours notice.
Do you offer home service?
Yes, we provide professional massage services in the comfort of your own home within our service areas.
Do you offer hotel service?
Absolutely. We serve guests at hotels across Cebu, bringing relaxation directly to your room.
What areas do you cover?
We primarily serve Cebu City, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu, Talisay, and neighboring municipalities. Additional areas may be available upon request.
Is there an additional travel fee?
Yes, a travel fee of ₱150–₱300 applies depending on your location. We'll confirm the exact fee when you book.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, GCash, PayMaya, and bank transfers. Credit cards are also accepted at our spa location.
Can I request a specific therapist?
Yes, you may request a preferred therapist. We'll do our best to accommodate your preference based on availability.
What is your cancellation policy?
We kindly ask for at least 2 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee.
