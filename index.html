Questions guests ask

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, we welcome walk-ins at our spa on M. C. Briones St., Cebu City. We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time and therapist.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are highly recommended, especially for home and hotel service. We accept same-day bookings with at least 2 hours notice.

Do you offer home massage service in Cebu?

Yes. A licensed therapist comes to your home in Cebu City, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu, Talisay and nearby areas. Home service has a minimum booking of 2 hours.

Do you offer hotel massage service in Cebu?

Yes. We serve guests at hotels across Cebu and bring the treatment to your room, with the same minimum booking of 2 hours.

What areas do you cover?

We primarily serve Cebu City, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu, Talisay and neighboring municipalities. Additional areas may be available on request.

Is there an additional travel fee?

Yes. A travel fee of ₱150–₱300 applies depending on your location. We confirm the exact fee when you book.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, GCash, PayMaya and bank transfers. Credit cards are also accepted at our spa location.

Can I request a specific therapist?

Yes. You may request a preferred therapist and we will do our best to accommodate you based on availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 2 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee.
